An interactive visual novel

Late Summer
Screening Room

Before an old school disappears, uncover a forgotten film, restore five lost memories, and decide how one unforgettable summer ends.

The Story

Return to the seaside town of Shiomizaka as Riku Sahara. In an abandoned projection room, you find a reel of film no one remembers making. Its images lead back to a summer shared by five friends, a vanished girl, and a memory that was deliberately left behind.

Read cinematic scenes, follow the clues between conversations, and choose what the unfinished film will become.

Your Choices Matter

  • Shape relationships and uncover hidden memories.
  • Explore branching paths and multiple endings.
  • Revisit unlocked turning points with the rewind feature.
  • Experience a nostalgic summer with original music and illustrated scenes.
