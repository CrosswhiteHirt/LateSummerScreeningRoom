Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer identified by NeilCook930159@outlook.com (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Late Summer Screening Room (the “App”). The App is an offline, story-driven game. We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal information.

Information We Handle

Game progress, settings, and unlocked content are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this local save data.

If you choose to watch an optional rewarded advertisement, the advertising provider may process technical information such as device type, operating system, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interaction data, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted. This processing is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy.

Advertising and Tracking

Advertisements are optional and are offered only when you choose a rewarded rewind in the App. Declining an advertisement, or an advertisement being unavailable, does not prevent you from continuing the core game.

We do not access your advertising identifier or request App Tracking Transparency permission in the current App release. If a future version requests permission to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, it will present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt first and update this policy before that change takes effect.

Sharing

We do not share local game-progress data. Information processed to deliver an optional advertisement may be received directly by the advertising provider and its service providers as necessary to provide, measure, secure, or limit the frequency of that advertisement.

Children

The App is not directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can address the request.

Security and Retention

Local save data remains on your device until you delete the App or remove the data through your device settings. We retain only information that is necessary to respond to support requests or meet legal obligations.

Your Choices and Contact

You can avoid optional ads by not selecting the rewarded rewind option. You can also manage advertising and privacy preferences through your device settings where available.

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact the developer at NeilCook930159@outlook.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App or applicable law changes. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.
