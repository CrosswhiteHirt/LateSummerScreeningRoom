## Description

The last summer in Shiomizaka is about to fade away.

Return to a seaside town as Riku Sahara and step inside an abandoned projection room where a film no one remembers making is waiting to be shown. Each reel reveals a fragment of a summer shared by five friends—and a memory someone chose to leave behind.

Late Summer Screening Room is a story-driven interactive visual novel about friendship, memory, and the ending of a film that was never finished. Read cinematic scenes, notice the details hidden between conversations, and make choices that shape relationships, reveal clues, and lead to multiple endings.

FEATURES
• A heartfelt coming-of-age mystery set in a nostalgic seaside town
• Meaningful choices, branching scenes, and multiple endings to discover
• A rewind feature that lets you revisit unlocked turning points
• Illustrated characters, cinematic backgrounds, and an original atmospheric soundtrack
• Local save progress so you can continue the story at your own pace

The projector is ready. What will you remember when the final reel begins?
